# Glossary

## Bounty

A reward attached to a stolen device to incentivize its return.

## DeviceState

The on-chain record for a registered device. It includes owner, hashed IMEI, model, stolen status, recovery contact, and optional insurer.

## Freighter

A Stellar wallet browser extension used for signing Stellar and Soroban transactions.

## Hashed IMEI

A SHA-256 hash of a device IMEI. Haven stores the hash, not the raw IMEI.

## IMEI

International Mobile Equipment Identity. A unique identifier for a mobile device.

## Killswitch

The Haven flow that marks a device as stolen and attaches a recovery bounty.

## Launchtube

A Stellar ecosystem tool for fee abstraction and sponsored transactions.

## Passkey Kit

A toolkit for WebAuthn/passkey-based onboarding.

## SEP-10

A Stellar Ecosystem Proposal for authentication with Stellar accounts.

## SEP-24

A Stellar Ecosystem Proposal for interactive fiat deposit and withdrawal flows through anchors.

## Soroban

Stellar's smart contract platform.

## USDC

A stablecoin issued by Circle. Haven plans to use USDC for stable recovery bounties.
